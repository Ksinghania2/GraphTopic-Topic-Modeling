GraphTopic

Graph-based topic extraction from large text corpora: word co-occurrence network → community detection → topics.

1 · Choose a corpus

Labelled synthetic news-style corpus across 10 subjects (space, medicine, sports, finance, AI, climate, food, politics, education, travel). Fast demo – completes in a few seconds and reports accuracy against the known labels.

2 · Parameters

Larger window → denser graph. Lower γ → fewer, broader topics; higher γ → more, finer topics.

How it works

1. Clean

Tokenise, remove stop-words, lemmatise, detect phrases.

2. Build graph

Words are nodes; edges carry NPMI of sliding-window co-occurrence.

3. Detect communities

Louvain finds densely connected word groups – each is a topic.

4. Infer

Documents receive topic distributions from word memberships.
